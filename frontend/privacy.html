Clinic AI Receptionist is an AI-powered virtual receptionist for clinics and similar businesses. It answers inbound calls, understands what a caller needs, checks availability, books and manages appointments, and passes calls to a human when required. This Privacy Policy explains what information the service handles, how that information is used to operate the service, and the choices available to clinics that use Clinic AI Receptionist and to the callers they serve.

This policy applies to the Clinic AI Receptionist dashboard, the AI call handling service, and the integrations a clinic chooses to connect. Clinics that use the service are responsible for informing their own callers and patients about call handling and for having any consent required in their jurisdiction.

Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably required to provide the service:

Account information

  • Name, email address, and a securely hashed password for each dashboard user.
  • The user's role within the workspace (for example owner, admin, receptionist, or analyst) and workspace membership.

Clinic / business information

  • Clinic or business name, branches or locations, time zone, and operating hours.
  • Services offered, providers or doctors, and the configuration used to run the AI receptionist.

Appointment information

  • Appointment date and time, the service and provider involved, status, and reminder state.
  • Notes and details entered by clinic staff or captured during a call.

Caller / patient information provided through the service

  • Information a caller provides during a call — such as name, phone number, and the reason for the call — and any additional details needed to schedule or handle the request.
  • Information clinic staff enter about a patient or lead in the dashboard.

Call-related information

  • Call metadata such as time, duration, direction, and the phone numbers involved.
  • Call transcripts, conversation or session data, call outcomes, and records of transfers to a human.
  • Call audio where recording is used by the telephony and voice-processing providers to deliver and improve call quality.

Usage information (where applicable)

  • Log data, approximate device and browser information, and IP address.
  • Actions taken in the dashboard, used for operating, securing, and troubleshooting the service.

Integration information

  • Authorization tokens and related account identifiers for services a clinic connects, such as Google.
  • Data returned by those integrations that is needed to perform the requested action — for example calendar availability and appointment or event details.

We do not seek to collect more information than is needed to run the service, and we do not engage in data practices beyond those described in this policy.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate and support the service, specifically to:

  • Provide AI receptionist functionality — understand caller requests and respond on the clinic's behalf.
  • Handle calls — answer, route, transcribe, and transfer calls to clinic staff when needed.
  • Schedule appointments — check availability, create, reschedule, cancel, and send reminders for appointments.
  • Support clinic configuration — apply each workspace's branches, services, providers, hours, and preferences.
  • Operate the service — maintain, monitor, debug, and improve reliability and performance.
  • Maintain security — authenticate users, prevent abuse and fraud, and protect the integrity of the service.
  • Send service communications — account, security, configuration, and support-related messages.

Google Calendar Integration

When a clinic connects a Google account, Clinic AI Receptionist may use Google Calendar access to:

  • Check availability against the connected calendar before offering appointment times.
  • Create appointments and calendar events for bookings made through the service.
  • Manage appointment and calendar information — such as updating or cancelling events — where that is supported.

A connected Google account can be disconnected at any time from the integration settings in the dashboard. Access can also be reviewed and revoked directly from your Google Account's third-party access controls at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the service can no longer read or write calendar data.

Clinic AI Receptionist does not use Google Calendar data for advertising, and does not sell it. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
